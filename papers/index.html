Honours project: CFXS — an 8-line USB channel bank

Final-year engineering honours project: a budget 8-line computer-to-telephone (FXS) interface — "nothing more than a glorified ADC/DAC device, the computer it's connected to does all the work" — aiming for a US$150 device that could put real phones through a whole house. Completed May 2007 with a live demonstration across eight telephones, graded 94% (High Distinction), and released as open source in 2008.
